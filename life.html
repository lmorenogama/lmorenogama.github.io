A little more about me

My life outside academia

Colombian roots, a curious soul.

I am proudly Colombian, from Bogotá, and I have called Colombia, Spain, the United Arab Emirates, and the United States home. I love travelling, discovering new places, and getting to know the people who make them feel alive.

I am also a foodie. I will happily try something new wherever I go—but Colombian food will always win. I recognize that this is a strong claim. I stand by it.

Good company, preferably with sunshine.

I love to laugh and spend time with people who feel like sunshine: warm, kind, and easy to be myself around. At home, I have a lovely cat, Cleopatra. Her name suggests royalty; I am not here to dispute that.

In motion, and in thought.

Cycling, hiking, or simply finding a way to move my body makes me happy. So does slowing down with something good to read.

I love thinking deeply, reading, and learning about psychology, human connections, and Stoic principles. A little perspective, a little movement, a good meal, and a lot of laughter: that is a pretty good day in my book.
