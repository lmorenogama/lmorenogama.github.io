Research in progress

Preliminary project overviews; findings are not reported here.

Green Taxes for Trust: The Limits of Earmarking in Post-Conflict Colombia.

This project examines carbon taxation in post-conflict Colombia, asking how earmarking tax revenues for specific purposes relates to public trust and support for environmental policy.

Which Wells Get Shut Down First? Environmental Compliance, Communities, and Oil Decommissioning in the Ecuadorian Amazon

With Paasha Mahdavi (UCSB).

This project asks how environmental compliance and local communities shape decisions about which oil wells are decommissioned in the Ecuadorian Amazon.

Discounting Tomorrow: Colombia’s Renewable Energy Ambitions Amid Conflict and Resource Struggles

With William Nomikos (UCSB) and Cesar Martinez (UCSB).

This project explores how conflict and struggles over resources intersect with Colombia’s renewable energy ambitions, focusing on the political and community dynamics of the energy transition.

Rebels and the Commons: Armed Group Governance and Common-Pool Resource Management in Colombia

With Zachariah Parcels (Purdue University).

This project examines the relationship between armed group governance and the management of shared natural resources in Colombia, asking how these forms of authority interact with community resource governance.
