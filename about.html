Politics, environment,
and community.

I am Laura Elizabeth Moreno-Gama, a PhD Candidate in Political Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara. I study the political economy of conflict, development, and environmental politics, with a focus on Latin America.

My work draws on experimental and quasi-experimental methods to examine questions about energy transitions, environmental governance, and communities. My fields are comparative politics, environmental politics, and quantitative methods.

I hold an MSc in Economics (2017–2019) and a BSc in Economics (2013–2017) from Universidad del Rosario in Bogotá, Colombia. I also spent an exchange semester at Universidad Complutense de Madrid in 2016–2017.

Before UCSB, I worked in research at Universidad del Rosario, New York University Abu Dhabi, and United Arab Emirates University, and as a Nuclear R&D Specialist at Nawah Energy Company.
