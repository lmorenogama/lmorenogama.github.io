I am a PhD Candidate in Political Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara, with interests in comparative politics, political economy, and environmental politics.

My research interests center on energy transitions, environmental governance, and the political economy of Latin America. My Colombian background informs my interest in the relationships between communities and their environments.
