Welcome. Bienvenidos.

I am Laura Elizabeth Moreno-Gama, a PhD Candidate in Political Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

I study the political economy of conflict, development, and environmental politics, with a focus on Latin America. My research examines energy transitions, environmental governance, and the communities at the heart of these changes.

Originally from Bogotá, Colombia, I bring a deep connection to the region to my work. I draw on experimental and quasi-experimental methods to explore these questions.
