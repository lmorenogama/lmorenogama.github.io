Welcome/Bienvenidxs

I am Laura, a PhD Candidate in Political Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

My research explores how communities influence environmental governance, shape public policy, and build—or challenge—state capacity in Latin America, particularly in Colombia and Ecuador. Working at the intersection of political economy and Indigenous politics, I use experimental and quasi-experimental methods to investigate these relationships. I hold an M.Sc and a B.Sc in Economics from Universidad del Rosario in Bogotá, Colombia. Before joining UCSB, I worked at New York University Abu Dhabi and United Arab Emirates University, and as a Nuclear R&D Specialist at ENEC/Nawah Energy Company.

I am a PhD affiliate of The 2035 Initiative and the Broom Center for Demography at UCSB.
